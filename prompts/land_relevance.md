# Land and Territorial Relevance

## Task

Determine whether the parliamentary speech is relevant to land and territorial issues.

## Definition

A speech is **RELEVANT** when it explicitly addresses, even briefly, issues related to:

- land conflicts;
- land regularization;
- agrarian reform;
- Indigenous land demarcation;
- recognition or titling of traditional or territorial lands;
- social movements, collective actors, or disputes directly associated with land and territorial rights;
- closely related land or territorial controversies.

A speech is **IRRELEVANT** when it does not substantively address these issues or related disputes.

## Decision rule

Classify the speech as **RELEVANT** whenever an identifiable connection to land, territorial rights, agrarian reform, land conflicts, or associated collective actors is present.

Otherwise, classify it as **IRRELEVANT**.
