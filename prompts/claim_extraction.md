# Claim Extraction

## Task

Extract all claims from the parliamentary speech that contribute to the delegitimization of land struggles, territorial rights, or the collective actors associated with them.

## Definition

A **claim** is a proposition, premise, judgment, evaluation, opinion, or proposal that expresses or supports a position on a substantive issue.

Claims may be explicit or may require contextual reconstruction when their meaning is distributed across multiple sentences.

Purely descriptive or trivial factual statements should not be extracted unless they perform an argumentative function within the speech.

## Analytical focus

The speeches analyzed here contest the legitimacy of land and territorial agendas, social struggles, claims, rights, or the continued recognition, protection, or expansion of land and territorial rights associated with traditional, Indigenous, rural, peasant, subsistence, riverine, forest, or other territorially organized communities.

Extract every claim that substantively contributes to this delegitimizing process.

## Instructions

For each speech:

1. identify all relevant claims;
2. preserve the substantive meaning of the speaker's position;
3. use the broader argumentative context when the claim cannot be reconstructed from a single sentence;
4. identify the context supporting each claim, including its argumentative premise and, when present, its justification.
